Questions The Monal will ask

Straight answers

Do we have to change our phone number?

No. Your published number stays exactly the same. Calls are routed to the AI — all day, or only after-hours/overflow in a hybrid setup where your host answers first and the AI catches what's missed.

What if a caller asks something the AI shouldn't answer?

It's role-locked: it only discusses The Monal. Anything else — news, other businesses, tech questions — gets a polite redirect back to reservations and dining. Complaints or unusual requests are escalated to a manager's number instantly.

Does it really speak Urdu?

Yes — natural spoken Pakistani Urdu, not translated text. It switches languages the moment the caller does. You'll hear it in the demo above.

Where do bookings go?

Three places at once: a live reservations dashboard for the front desk, an auto-written booking sheet (Excel/Google Sheets), and a WhatsApp confirmation to the guest. Your team changes nothing about how they work.

What happens during load-shedding or internet issues at the restaurant?

The AI runs in the cloud, not on restaurant hardware — it keeps answering even if the branch is offline. Bookings sync to the dashboard the moment connectivity returns.

How long does setup take?

The pilot goes live within one week: we load your menu, timings and packages, tune the voice, connect the number, and test with your team before a single real customer calls.
